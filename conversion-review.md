## Evidence and limits

- Not available: real activity sheets, verified counts, customer testimonials, acquisition ads, funnel data, exact delivery timing and support contact.

1. Product-first: “Kit Prático de Alfabetização” with “Escolha a habilidade. Imprima. Pratique com a criança.”
2. Organization-first: “Atividades de alfabetização organizadas por habilidade”.
3. Offer-first: “Kit Essencial: atividades e quatro complementos por R$ 19,90”.

Selected approach 1: identifies the product and describes its use without an unsupported outcome. Approach 3 appears in the offer sections. Ad-message matching remains unverified because ads were not supplied.

## Learning hypothesis
Clearer practical use, visible contents and explicit purchase conditions may increase checkout-link clicks per sales-page visit. This is a hypothesis, not a measured result.

Compare with a prior period only under comparable ad traffic and acquisition conditions. Track checkout-link click rate separately from confirmed purchases; protect purchase conversion and refund rate. Without adequate comparable visits and reliable events, treat the outcome as inconclusive. Do not mark checkout clicks as purchases.
